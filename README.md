# Congeria
Congeria (Latin for chaos) casts you as a cop, a thief, or a joker influencer secretly planted on one side, clearing minigames to unlock weapons and crack (or protect) the vault. But beware the BOUNCE BACK: kill someone and they swap characters with you. ENJOY!!!!

Congeria means chaos in latin. That is the exact embodiment of our game.
You start with the option to pick one of 3 characters - a police, thief and influencer acting as a joker.
You have to complete a series of minigames to complete your mission. The thief needs to steal from the vault and escape and the cop needs to catch the thief red handed.
The influencer meanwhile acts as a joker, he is randomly placed in either the thief or the cop’s side, where he acts as an ally or spy.
Each character comes with their own specialised custom abilities. After completing one task, you can unlock a knife or gun. 
The key part of the game however is the BOUNCE BACK feature, when you kill someone the dead person “bounces back” and swaps character with you.
ENJOY!!!!
